## What is retired, and what is not

Retired: the unconditional binding that recomputed the assembled `body_sha256` of the four measured carriers at committed `HEAD` on every `make test` run and aborted on drift, plus its CI consequence.

Not retired: the evidence itself. `specs/023-agent-native-repo-borrowing/evidence/` keeps every raw record, regrade, manifest, and runner exactly as committed. The register rows for r9/r11 remain valid as of-that-round facts. Anyone can still read the contract to see what was pinned.

Consequence to state plainly: with the `Makefile` recipe no longer carrying the C3 command, `test_red_baseline_023_c3_regrade.rb` can no longer verify itself — `make_test_candidate_invocation?` requires that exact invocation and will abort. The contract is retired, not merely unwired; it is kept committed as the readable record of what the gate enforced, not as a runnable check.

### Declined: re-wiring a reduced frozen-evidence integrity checker

Independent review, given the checker's full source, proposed extracting the surviving Group C and Group E assertions into a reduced deterministic checker and wiring that into `make test` before retiring the original. The proposal is coherent and its cost would be low — it pins evidence files, not skill carriers, so it would not reintroduce the refresh tax. It is declined anyway, on this repository's own rule rather than on convenience.

`design-review-gate-mechanics.md`'s trust-model leg names the exact shape being proposed: machinery that only defends against adversaries the trust model already excludes, with **content digests the author can freely regenerate** as its worked example, is redundant detection at full complexity cost. A digest checker over the frozen records is that shape. The party able to rewrite `evidence/*.json` is the party able to update the pins in the same commit — which is not hypothetical, since the retired contract's own recovery path required relanding `CANONICAL_FILE_SHA256` on every legitimate refresh. Against that actor the checker adds no detection; against an accidental rewrite, Git already surfaces the change as a diff on frozen files, and the reduced checker would surface it as a slightly larger diff on frozen files plus pins.

The reviewer's counter — that `AGENTS.md` instructions and visible diffs are review-dependent rather than mechanical — is accurate and is the residual risk this round accepts, stated as such: after this landing, a rewrite of the round-023 records is caught by review of a visible diff, not by a gate. That is the same protection every other frozen artifact in this repository has, and the records now back no live claim. Re-wiring machinery whose only real function is to make a visible diff slightly more visible would repeat the design mistake this round exists to undo.

If a future round wants mechanical protection for historical evidence, the durable form is a general frozen-path rule applied to every such directory, owned by the repository's contract-coverage gate — not a bespoke checker for one retired round.
